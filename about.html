About

A connector across sales, success, and strategy.

The cross-functional perspective that comes from working customer marketing, customer experience, and direct account ownership—all inside one fast-growing B2B SaaS company.

Over the past three years, I’ve contributed to the successful private equity exit of a B2B SaaS company. We grew from 300 to over 700 employees, acquired smaller companies to expand our product portfolio, and built and refined hundreds of sales processes along the way.

At this company, I worked in Customer Marketing—focusing on community building, digital experience build-out, event coordination, and ABM. I moved into Customer Success Management to gain experience with direct account ownership.

The combination of understanding the entire customer lifecycle, and various forms of communication from the perspective of numerous departments—including sales, CS, CX, product, and marketing—has given me a unique, comprehensive outlook on the ultimate customer experience.

That perspective doesn’t just allow me to provide a robust support experience to my customers; it makes me a particularly strong team member in a start-up environment. I commonly act as a connecting agent between departments, facilitate cross-collaboration, and am pulled into organizational initiatives to build out strategies that bridge multiple teams.

Above all, I love to be involved—and to get people talking and sharing ideas.
