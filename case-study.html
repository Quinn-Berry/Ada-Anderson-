The Challenge 01

A competitor was quietly winning my mid-market book.

As a customer success manager at a late-stage startup, I noticed a competitor gaining traction with several of my mid-market accounts—putting roughly $2M in renewals at risk over the next year. The signals were quiet at first: a renewal pushed, a stakeholder going cold, a sudden interest in feature parity. But the pattern was real.

Before I could fix anything, I had to figure out what specifically made these accounts vulnerable.

The Diagnosis 02

Mapping the churn signals.

I worked through the at-risk cohort to identify the risk factors that consistently appeared—the early indicators that an account was likely to convert into a churn conversation rather than a renewal.

  • IndustryVerticals where the competitor had stronger positioning.
  • Company sizeMid-market range where the competitor priced aggressively.
  • Number of locationsMulti-site complexity created a feature-parity narrative.
  • Support sentimentRecent CSAT/Support trend data, surfaced via Gainsight.

Then I set the goal: reduce at-risk revenue by 30% within one quarter—a target tight enough to demand executive attention and tangible enough to mobilize teams beyond CS.

The Play 03

A coordinated competitive response, run across three teams.

CS alone wasn’t going to win this. I partnered with Account Management, Marketing, and Customer Experience to launch a targeted competitive campaign sequenced across the 90-day window.

  • i.
    Tailored battlecards Built with Marketing and Product Marketing—mapping our differentiators directly to the competitor’s pitch by industry and use case, and arming AMs with answers to the specific objections we were hearing.
  • ii.
    Proactive executive outreach Sequenced executive sponsor touchpoints into the most vulnerable accounts—not waiting for the renewal conversation, but reframing the relationship before competitive doubt could solidify.
  • iii.
    Improved onboarding touchpoints Partnered with CX to tighten onboarding and early-lifecycle touchpoints for vulnerable customers, addressing the support-sentiment risk factor at its source.
The Outcome 04

85% stabilized in 90 days—and a measurable NPS lift on the way out.

Within 90 days, we stabilized 85% of the flagged accounts—well past the 30% reduction target. We saw a measurable lift in NPS among the previously at-risk customers, suggesting the response did more than defend revenue: it repaired sentiment.
