About

A connector across sales, success, and strategy.

Over the past three years, I’ve contributed to the successful private equity exit of a B2B SaaS company that grew from 300 to over 700 employees, acquired smaller companies to expand the product portfolio, and built and refined hundreds of sales processes along the way.

I started in Customer Marketing—focused on community building, digital experience, event coordination, and ABM—and moved into Customer Success Management to gain experience with direct account ownership. Understanding the entire customer lifecycle from the perspective of sales, CS, CX, product, and marketing has given me a comprehensive outlook on what a great customer experience actually looks like.

I commonly act as a connecting agent between departments — pulled into organizational initiatives to build strategies that bridge multiple teams.

That perspective doesn’t just shape how I support my customers; it makes me a particularly strong team member in start-up environments. Above all, I love to get involved—and to get people talking and sharing ideas.

Case study

Saving $2M in at-risk renewals.

A competitor was gaining traction with mid-market accounts. Within 90 days, cross-functional execution turned a churn threat into a stabilized book of business—and a measurable lift in customer sentiment.

01 — Signal

Identify the threat

Noticed a competitor gaining traction with mid-market accounts, putting roughly $2M in renewals at risk over the next year. Mapped risk factors—industry, company size, number of locations, and support sentiment—to pinpoint churn signals.

02 — Strategy

Set the goal & partner

Set a target to reduce at-risk revenue by 30% within one quarter. Partnered across Account Management, Marketing, and Customer Experience to launch a coordinated competitive response.

03 — Execution

Run the play

Launched a targeted competitive campaign with tailored battlecards, proactive executive outreach, and improved onboarding touchpoints for vulnerable customers—all sequenced over 90 days.
